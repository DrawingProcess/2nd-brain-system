US stocks closed a volatile week higher on Friday, July 31, 2026, even as the 10-year
Treasury yield jumped to 4.73%, its highest level since January 2025, on investor
selling. The Nasdaq Composite rose about 1%, the Dow rose 0.5%, and the S&P 500 added
0.7%, with the Dow also posting a 0.7% gain for July. Amazon surged 15% on an earnings
beat and chip-business growth, while Apple fell 7% after Services and China revenue
missed -- a mixed follow-through from Microsoft's historic 15% rally the day before.
The four hyperscalers (Amazon, Microsoft, Meta, Alphabet) guided to combined 2026
capex of $720-745 billion. Oil rose on renewed Strait of Hormuz tensions (WTI ~$85,
Brent ~$90), and University of Michigan sentiment data showed a broad pickup despite
high energy costs.
