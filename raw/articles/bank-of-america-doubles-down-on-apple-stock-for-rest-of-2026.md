Bank of America is maintaining a bullish stance on Apple stock for the remainder of 2026, doubling down on its positive outlook. The firm's analysts believe investors are misreading Apple's June quarter results and September quarter guidance. They argue that concerns about business slowing may be overstated, suggesting the stock remains a compelling investment opportunity.
