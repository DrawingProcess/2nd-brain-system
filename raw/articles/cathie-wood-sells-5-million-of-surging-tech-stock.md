Cathie Wood's Ark Investment Management has sold $5.5 million worth of Snowflake stock after a recent 10% surge in the share price. Wood is known for locking in gains when her technology holdings experience significant rallies. This move comes ahead of Snowflake's earnings report, suggesting portfolio rebalancing to manage risk exposure.
