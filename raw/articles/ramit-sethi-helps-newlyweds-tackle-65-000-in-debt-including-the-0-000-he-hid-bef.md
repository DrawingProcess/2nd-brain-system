Financial expert Ramit Sethi is helping newlyweds tackle $165,000 in debt, including $30,000 that one spouse hid before their wedding. Sethi emphasizes that the core issue is not the money itself but the breach of trust between partners. He has developed a plan to address both the financial and relational aspects of their debt situation.
