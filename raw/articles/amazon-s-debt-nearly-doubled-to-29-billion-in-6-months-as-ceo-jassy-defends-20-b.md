Amazon's debt has nearly doubled to $129 billion over the past six months as the company aggressively invests in AI infrastructure. CEO Andy Jassy is defending the massive $220 billion annual spending on data centers, which includes $53.1 billion in AI-driven capital projects in just one quarter. This reflects Amazon's strategic priority to maintain leadership in cloud computing and artificial intelligence services.
