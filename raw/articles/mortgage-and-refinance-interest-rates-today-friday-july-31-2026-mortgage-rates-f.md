The average 30-year fixed mortgage rate was 6.55% on Friday, July 31, 2026, down 10
basis points from the prior day. The 15-year fixed rate stood at 6.03% (-4 bps), and
the 5/1 ARM at 6.42% (-16 bps) -- the article frames the day's move as rates finding
more room to fall.
