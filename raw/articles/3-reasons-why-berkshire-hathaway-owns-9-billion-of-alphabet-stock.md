Berkshire Hathaway has built a $29 billion position in Alphabet stock, representing one of its largest technology investments. The conglomerate appears to view Alphabet as undervalued despite its $4.1 trillion market capitalization. This substantial stake reflects Warren Buffett's confidence in the company's long-term prospects and competitive position in the technology sector.
