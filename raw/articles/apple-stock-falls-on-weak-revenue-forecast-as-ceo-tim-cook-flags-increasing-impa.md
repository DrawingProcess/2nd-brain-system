Apple reported fiscal Q3 2026 earnings on Thursday, July 30, 2026, beating analyst
expectations on both revenue and EPS on strong iPhone sales. The stock fell anyway
after Services and Greater China revenue came in below expectations; CEO Tim Cook
cited an "increasing impact" from a memory-chip shortage on the outlook.
