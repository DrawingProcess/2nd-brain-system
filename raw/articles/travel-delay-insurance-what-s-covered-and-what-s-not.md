Travel delay insurance can reimburse unexpected costs such as meals and lodging
caused by a covered delay, but the article's teaser notes two common gaps: short
delays (below the policy's minimum-hour threshold) and luxury-tier expenses are
typically excluded. Full article body not accessible beyond this teaser.
