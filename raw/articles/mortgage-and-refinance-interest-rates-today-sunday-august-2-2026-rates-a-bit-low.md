Mortgage rates have declined slightly compared to last week, offering some relief to potential homebuyers and refinancers. The 30-year fixed rate fell 10 basis points to 6.65%, while the 15-year fixed rate dropped 3 basis points to 6.01%. The 5/1 ARM rate rose by one basis point to 6.65%, showing mixed movement across different mortgage products.
